## Ancient Greek meaning

σφέτερ-ος, α, ον, possessive Adj. of the 3rd pers. pl. σφεῖς, their own, their, Il. 17.287 , Od. 1.274 , al., Hes. Th. 155 , Pi. P. 10.38 ; strengthd., αὐτῶν σφετέρῃσιν ἀτασθαλίῃσιν Od. 1.7 ; rare in Com., Ar. Ra. 1464 , Fr. 350 ; in Prose the gen. ἑαυτῶν is commonly used, but σφέτερος also occurs, Th. 3.95 , 7.1 , IG 12.29.6 , 57.46 , Pl. Euthd. 304c ; τὰ ς. their own property, Th. 2.20 , X. HG 5.3.12 ; ὅσοι τὰ ς. φρονοῦντες . . περιῆσαν Th. 3.68 , cf. X. HG 7.5.5 ; τὸν ς. (sc. φόβον) their own (fear), Th. 6.36 ; τὸ ς. αὐτῶν their own business, Pl. Sph. 243b ; τὸ ς. αὐτῶν συμφέρον their own interest, Arist. Pol. 1296a36 ; ἀπὸ τῆς ς. αὐτῶν (sc. χώρας) X. Ath. 2.5 , cf. IG 12(1).977.16 (Carpathos, iv B.C.); νόμοις χρῆσθαι τοῖς ς. αὐτῶν ib. 22.1.15 ; οἱ ς. their own people, Th. 6.71 , X. HG 2.4.18 . also of the 3rd pers. sg., his or her own, his, her, for ἑός, ὅς, Hes. Sc. 90 , Pi. O. 13.61 , P. 4.83 , A. Ag. 760 (lyr.), Pers. 900 (lyr.), and in later Prose, Plb. 7.14.3 , etc. sts. also used of other persons: of 2nd pl., = ὑμέτερος , your own, your, Il. 9.327 (sed leg. μαρναμένοις), Hes. Op. 2 , A.R. 4.1327 , AP 9.134 ; cf. σφεῖς B. 111 . of 2nd dual, Alcm. 3 . of 2nd sg., = σός , thine own, only in Theoc. 22.67 . of 1st sg., = ἐμός , mine own, Id. 25.163 . of 1st pl., = ἡμέτερος , our own, X. Cyr. 6.1.10 codd., A.R. 4.1353 , Plb. 11.4.3 , 11.31.6 , App. Mith. 5 , etc. --Cf. σφός.[^lsj]
