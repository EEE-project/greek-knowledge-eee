## Ancient Greek meaning

τρίτα^τος [ι^], η, ον, lengthd. poet. for τρίτος, like μέσσατος for μέσος, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 1.252, **[8th c. BC]** 14.117, **[5th c. BC]** E. Hipp. 135(lyr.), **[3rd c. BC]** A.R. 1.53; τριτάτην, abs., in the third place,IG 4.682.14(Hermione: so Boeckh; τρίτατ' ἦν Fraenkel).[^lsj]
