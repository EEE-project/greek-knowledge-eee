## Ancient Greek meaning

μηκ-άς, άδος, ἡ, bleating one, in Hom. always of goats, in pl., μ. αἶγες Il. 11.383 , Od. 9.124 , 244 , al., cf. Antiph. 1 , 52.8 ; also μ. ἄρνες E. Cyc. 189 . as Subst., = αἴξ , S. Fr. 509 , AP 9.123 (< Leon. >); λευκὴ μ. Luc. D DMeretr. 7.1 : pl., Theoc. 1.87 , 5.100 .[^lsj]
