## Ancient Greek meaning

 αὐτάρ ( ἀvυτάρ IG 12.1012), Conj. = ἀτάρ (Ep. and Cypr., Inscr.Cypr. 57 H.):— but, besides, moreover, prop. to introduce a contrast, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 13.286, al.; also to mark a rapid succession of details, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 2.406, al.; opp. μέν, Ἥφαιστος μέν . . αὐ. ἄρα Ζεύς . . ib. **[8th c. BC]** 102 sq., cf. **[8th c. BC]** Od. 19.512 sq.; **[8th c. BC]** αὐτάρ τοι but nevertheless, Il. 15.45.—In an Epic reminiscence, **[5th c. BC]** Hermipp. 63.17 (hex.). αὐτάρ· αὐτομάτη, ἑκουσία, **[5th c. AD]** Hsch. [^lsj]
