## Ancient Greek meaning

ἐϋκτίμενος [ι^], η, ον, ʽκτίζὠ = εὖ ναιόμενος , good to dwell in, epith. of cities, ἐϋ. πτολίεθρον Il. 2.501 , etc.; πόλις B. 5.149 ; of anything on which man's labour has been bestowed, νῆσον ἐϋκτιμένην ἐκάμοντο wrought it so as to be good to dwell in, Od. 9.130 ; ἐϋ. ἐν ἀλωῇ on a well-made threshing-floor, Il. 20.496 , 21.77 ; the same phrase, of a garden, well-wrought, Od. 24.226 . (εὐκτ- in h.Ap. 36 , B. l.c.)[^lsj]
