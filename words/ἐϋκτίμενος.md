## Ancient Greek meaning

 ἐϋκτίμενος [ι^], η, ον, ʽκτίζὠ = εὖ ναιόμενος , good to dwell in, epith. of cities, **[8th c. BC]** ἐϋ. πτολίεθρον Il. 2.501, etc.; **[5th c. BC]** πόλις B. 5.149; of anything on which man's labour has been bestowed, νῆσον ἐϋκτιμένην ἐκάμοντο wrought it so as to be good to dwell in, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 9.130; ἐϋ. ἐν ἀλωῇ on a well-made threshing-floor, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 20.496, **[8th c. BC]** 21.77; the same phrase, of a garden, well-wrought, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 24.226. (εὐκτ- in **[7th c. BC]** h.Ap. 36, **[5th c. BC]** B. l.c.) [^lsj]
