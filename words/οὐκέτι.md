## Ancient Greek meaning

οὐκέτι or οὐκ ἔτι, Adv. no more, no longer, no further: and generally, not now, opp. οὔπω (not yet), freq. inHom.,Hes., **[5th c. BC]** Hdt., and ; **[8th c. BC, Att.]** οὐκέτι πάμπαν Il. 13.701; **[8th c. BC]** οὐκέτι πάγχυ 19.343; with a word between, **[8th c. BC]** οὐ πάμπαν ἔτι 13.7; **[8th c. BC]** οὐ γὰρ ἔτι 2.13, **[8th c. BC]** 141, etc.—Sts. reversely, **[5th c. BC]** ἔτ' οὐκ S. Tr. 161, cf. **[5th c. BC]** ἔτ' οὐδέν Id. Ph. 1217(lyr.); **[4th c. BC]** ἔτ' οὐδείς Ar. Pl. 1177.[^lsj]
