## Ancient Greek meaning

πολύ-τροπος, ον, (τρέπω) much-turned, i.e. much-travelled, much-wandering, epith. of Odysseus, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 1.1, **[8th c. BC]** 10.330. turning many ways: metaph., shifty, versatile, wily, of Hermes, **[7th c. BC]** h.Merc. 13, **[7th c. BC]** 439; **[4th c. BC]** τοῖς ἀσθενέσι καὶ π. θηρίοις Pl. Plt. 291b; and in this sense Plato took the word as applied to Odysseus, **[4th c. BC]** Hp.Mi. 364e( Sup. ), al.; τὸ π. τῆς γνώμης their versatility of mind, **[4th c. BC]** Th. 3.83; τὸ π., of Alcibiades, **[1st c. AD]** Plu. Alc. 24. fickle, **[1st c. AD]** ὅμιλος Ps.-Phoc. 95. of diseases, changeful, complicated, **[1st c. AD]** Plu. Num. 22; also **[1st c.
