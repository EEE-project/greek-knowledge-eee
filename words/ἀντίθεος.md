## Ancient Greek meaning

ἀντίθεος, η, ον, equal to the gods, godlike (cf. **[2nd c. AD]** S.E. M. 7.6): Homeric epith. of heroes, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 5.663, etc.; of nations, ib. **[8th c. BC]** 12.408, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 6.241; of women, only ib. **[8th c. BC]** 11.117; applied even to Polyphemus and the suitors, ib. **[8th c. BC]** 1.70, **[8th c. BC]** 14.18; **[5th c. BC]** ἥρωες ἀ. B. 10.79. contrary to God, **[3rd/2nd c. BC]** Ph. 1.566, al. Subst. ἀντίθεος, ὁ, hostile deity, **[3rd c. AD]** Hld. 4.7, **[4th c. AD]** Iamb. Myst. 3.31,PMag.Lond. 121.635(unless Adj., disguised as a god).[^lsj]
