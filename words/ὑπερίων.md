## Ancient Greek meaning

 Ὑπερίων [ι—], ονος, ὁ, Hyperion, in Hom. the Sun-god: he always joins ʽγπερίων Ἠέλιος ( **[8th c. BC]** Il. 8.480, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 1.8, al.), or Ἠέλιος ʽ υπερίων ( **[8th c. BC]** Od. 12.133), exc. **[8th c. BC]** Il. 19.398, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 1.24, **[7th c. BC]** h.Ap. 369, where Ὑπερίων stands alone for Ἥλιος; in **[7th c. BC]** h.Hom. 31.4 Ὑπερίων is father of Ἥλιος. ʽγπερίων (derived from ὑπὲρ ἰών) is **[6th/5th c. BC]** Pythag. name for **[6th/5th c. BC]** 9, Theol.Ar. 58. [^lsj]
