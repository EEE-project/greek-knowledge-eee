## Ancient Greek meaning

μόρος, ὁ, (μείρομαι A) = μοῖρα111.1, fate, destiny, poet. and Ion. Prose: c. inf., μόρος [ἐστὶν] ὀλέσθαι 'tis my doom to die, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 19.421; ὑπὲρ μόρον beyond destiny, of those who by their own fault add to their destined share of misery, **[8th c. BC]** 20.30, **[8th c.
