## Ancient Greek meaning

ὀρφν-αῖος, α, ον, dark, murky, inHom.always epith. of night, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 10.83, al., **[5th c. BC]** E. Or. 1225, etc.; ὀρφναίη (sc. νύξ) **[3rd c. BC]** A.R. 2.670; **[5th c. BC]** φανέντος ὀρφναίου πυρός in the darkness, A. Ag. 21.[^lsj]
