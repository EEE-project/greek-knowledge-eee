## Ancient Greek meaning

ἐς, Ion. , old , Att. Dor. form of εἰς: all compounds must be sought under εἰσ-, except a few words which appear only in the form ἐσ-. **[4th c. AD]** Arc., = ἐκ ,IG 5(2).6.49(Tegea, iv B. C.), al. ; also Delph.,BCH 23.611, and Arg.,IG 4.492(Mycenae, vi B. C.) ; v. ἐκ init.[^lsj]
