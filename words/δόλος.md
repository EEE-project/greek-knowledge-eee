## Ancient Greek meaning

δόλος (A), ὁ, prop. bait for fish, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 12.252: hence, any cunning contrivance for deceiving or catching, as the net in which Hephaestus catches Ares, **[8th c. BC]** 8.276; the Trojan horse, ib. **[8th c. BC]** 494; Ixion's bride, **[5th c. BC]** Pi. P. 2.39; the robe of Penelope, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 19.137(pl.); ξύλινος δ. mouse-trap,Batr. 116. generally, any trick or stratagem, **[8th c. BC]** πυκινὸν δ. ἄλλον ὕφαινε Il. 6.187, etc.: in pl., wiles, **[8th c. BC]** δόλοι καὶ μήδεα 3.202; **[8th c. BC]** δόλοισι κεκασμένε 4.339, etc. in the abstract, craft, cunning, treachery, **[8th c. BC]** δόλῳ ἠὲ βίῃφι Od. 9.406; **[8th c. BC]** ἔπεφνε δόλῳ, οὔ τι κράτεΐ γε Il. 7.142; οὐ κατ' ἰσχὺν . . δόλῳ δέ . . **[5th c. BC]** A. Pr. 215, cf. **[5th c. BC]** Ch. 556, etc.; δόλοις ib. **[5th c. BC]** 888, **[5th c. BC]** S. OT 960, etc.; **[5th c. BC]** ἐκ δόλου Id. El. 279; **[5th c. BC]** ἐν δόλῳ Id. Ph. 102; **[5th c. BC]** σὺν δόλῳ A. Pers. 775; **[4th c.
