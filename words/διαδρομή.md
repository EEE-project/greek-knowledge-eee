## Ancient Greek meaning

διαδρομ-ή, ἡ, (διαδραμεῖν) running to and fro through a city, A. Th. 351 (pl.), cf. Hp. Epid. 7.122 , Plb. 15.30.2 ; αἱ δ. τῶν ἀστέρων shooting, Arist. Mete. 341a33 , al.; διαδρομὰς ὀξείας ἔχειν spread rapidly, of disease, Plu. 2.825d . running across, Antipho 3.4.4 ; passage through, σπιλάδος Plu. 2.476a . race (perh. team-race) or parade, OGI 339.36 (Sestos), 764.24 (Pergam.), SIG 694.56 (Elaea, ii B. C.). a cavalry manoeuvre, Anon. ap. Suid. Medic., Medic., δ. πνευμάτων, = βορβορυγμός , Dsc. 5.45 ; sensation, δ. νυγματώδης, φρικώδης, Sor. 2.17 , Philum. Ven. 17.1 . course, ἡμέρα δωδεκάωρος δ. Secund. Sent. 4 . place for running through, passage, X. Cyn. 10.8 ; of fish-ponds, δ. ἰχθυοτρόφοι Plu. Luc. 39 .[^lsj]
