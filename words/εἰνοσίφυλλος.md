## Ancient Greek meaning

εἰνοσίφυλλος [ι^], ον, (ἔνοσις) with quivering foliage, of wooded mountains, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 2.632, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 9.22, etc.[^lsj]
