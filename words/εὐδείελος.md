## Ancient Greek meaning

 εὐδείελος, ον, clear, distinct, Hom. (only in Od.), usu. of Ithaca, **[8th c. BC]** 2.167, **[8th c. BC]** 9.21, etc.: generally, **[8th c. BC]** ἦ πού τις νήσων εὐδείελος 13.234; farseen, **[5th c. BC]** Κρόνιον Pi. O. 1.111; ἄστυ Orac. ap. **[4th c. AD]** Eus. PE 6.7. open to the sun, sunny, **[5th c. BC]** χθὼν Ἰαολκοῦ Pi. P. 4.76; **[7th c. BC]** Κρίση h.Ap. 438; **[3rd c. BC]** ὅσσα φύει εὐ. αἶα Euph. 50. (In signf. **[3rd c. BC]** 1 perh. fr. δέελος (dub. l.), δῆλος (Δεα^λος?): the alternative expl. from δείλη (cf. supr. **[3rd c. BC]** 11) given by ancient Gramm., e.g. **[1st/2nd c. AD]** Apollon. Lex. and **[1st c. BC]** Str. 9.2.41, does not suit signf. **[1st c. BC]** 1, but suits the forms.) [^lsj]
