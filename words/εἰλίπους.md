## Ancient Greek meaning

εἰλίπους [ι^], ὁ, ἡ, πουν, τό , gen. ποδος· (εἴλω, πούς):— rolling in their gait, inHom.(only in dat. and acc. pl., **[8th c. BC]** Il. 6.424, **[8th c. BC]** 9.466) as epith. of oxen, which bring round their hind legs with a circling or rolling motion, cf. **[5th c. BC]** Hp. Art. 8; εἰλίποδες, abs., for oxen or kine, **[3rd c. BC]** Theoc. 25.131; also of women, having a rolling gait, **[5th c. BC]** Eup. 161; also expld. sens. obsc., **[6th c. BC]** Anacr. 164, cf.Paus.Gr. Fr. 154; cf. ἀνελλίπους.[^lsj]
