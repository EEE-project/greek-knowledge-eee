## Ancient Greek meaning

ἠρι^-γένεια, ἡ, (ἦρι, γενέσθαι) early-born, child of morn, epith. of Ἠώς, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 4.195, etc.: also abs.,= Ἠώς, **[8th c. BC]** 22.197, **[8th c. BC]** 23.347; καθαρᾶς ἅπερ ἠριγενείας as at clear morn, **[3rd c. BC]** Theoc. 24.39; γενέθλιον ἠριγένειαν a birthday morning,AP 9.353(Leon. **[4th c. BC]** Alex.); later, epith. of the Moon,Hymn.Mag. 5.3. in later , Ep. a day, **[4th c. AD]** Nonn. D. 38.271, **[4th c. AD]** Q.S. 10.478. (ἔαρ) bearing in spring, **[5th c. BC]** λέαινα A. Fr. 426(nisi leg. ἠυγ-).[^lsj]
