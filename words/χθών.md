## Ancient Greek meaning

χθών, ἡ, gen. χθονός, earth, esp. the surface of it (rarely soil, χθονὸς τρίμοιρον χλαῖναν A. Ag. 872 ): poet. word (Com. only in lyr. or paratrag.), very rare in Prose, LXX 3 Ki. 14.15 (cod. Alex. ), Supp.Epigr. 2.520 (Rome); seldom with Art.
