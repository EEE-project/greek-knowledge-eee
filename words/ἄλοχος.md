## Ancient Greek meaning

ἄλοχος [α^], ου, ἡ, (ἀ-copul., λέχος) poet., partner of one's bed, wife, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 1.114, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 3.403, al., **[5th c. BC]** A. Pers. 63, **[5th c. BC]** S. OT 181, **[5th c. BC]** E. Fr. 543, etc., cf. **[4th c. BC]** Arist. Pol. 1253b7; **[4th c. BC]** ἄλοχον εἰς δόμους ἄγειν Theodect. 13. leman, concubine, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 9.336, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 4.623. (ἀ- priv.) unwedded, ἄ. οὖσα τὴν λοχείαν εἴληχε, of Artemis, **[4th c. BC]** Pl. Tht. 149b, cf. Porph.ad **[8th c. BC]** Il. 11.155.[^lsj]
