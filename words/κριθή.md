## Ancient Greek meaning

κρι—θ-ή, ἡ, mostly in pl., barleycorns, barley (cf. κρῖ), the meal being ἄλφιτα: **[8th c. BC]** πυρῶν ἢ κριθῶν Il. 11.69, cf. **[8th c. BC]** Od. 9.110, **[8th c. BC]** 19.112, **[5th c. BC]** Ar. Eq. 1101;κριθᾶν μέδιμμνον IG 42(1).40.7(Epid.); **[3rd c. BC]** "τὰς οὐλοχύτας φέρε δεῦρο"—"τοῦτο δ' ἐστὶ τί;"—"κριθαί" Strato Com. 1.35; οἶνος ἐκ κριθέων πεποιημένος a kind of beer, **[5th c. BC]** Hdt. 2.77; **[5th c. BC]** ἐκ κριθῶν μέθυ A. Supp. 953, cf. **[4th c. BC]** Arist. Fr. 106; κριθαὶ πεφρυγμέναι, = κάχρυς , **[4th c. BC]** Th. 6.22, cf. Moer. **[4th c. BC]** p.213P.: pl., also of species of barley, **[3rd c. BC]** Thphr. HP 8.1.1: sg.,PGrenf. 2.29.9(ii B. C.); κ. Ἰνδική millet, Sorghum halepense, **[3rd c. BC]** Thphr. HP 8.4.2. pustule on the eyelid, stye, **[5th c. BC]** Hp. Epid. 2.2.5, **[2nd c. AD]** Gal. 12.742. barley-corn, the smallest weight, **[3rd c. BC]** Thphr. Lap. 46. in sg., = πόσθη , **[5th c. BC]** Ar. Pax 965. (The connexion with Lat.hordeum, OHG.gersta is doubtful.)[^lsj]
