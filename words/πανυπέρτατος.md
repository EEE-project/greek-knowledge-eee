## Ancient Greek meaning

παν-υ^πέρτατος, η, ον, highest of all, **[4th c. BC]** μεγέθει π. Arist. Mu. 397a15; π. ἐρρίζωνται, of trees, are rooted highest on the mountain, **[3rd c. BC]** A.R. 1.1122: hence, most remote or farthest from land, π. εἰν ἁλὶ κεῖται, of Ithaca, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 9.25. supreme, **[3rd c. BC]** Call. Jov. 91,Orph. H. 19.2,al.[^lsj]
