BC]** πυρὸς φέγγος A. Ch. 1037; **[5th c. BC]** Γᾶ S. Ant. 339 (lyr.). of persons, immortal, of the gods, **[7th c. BC]** h.Merc. 326; **[7th c. BC]** Στύξ Hes. Th. 389, **[7th c.
