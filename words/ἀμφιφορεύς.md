## Ancient Greek meaning

ἀμφιφορεύς, gen. έως, Ep. ῆος , ὁ: (φέρω, φορέω):— large jar or pitcher with two handles, of gold, Il. 23.92 , Od. 24.74 ; of stone, 13.105 ; for wine, 2.290 , etc.; for oil, Simon. 155.4 : used as cinerary urn, Il. l.c. = μετρητής , Theopomp.Hist. 374 . (The later form was ἀμφορεύς, q.v.)[^lsj]
