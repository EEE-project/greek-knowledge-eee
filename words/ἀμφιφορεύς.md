## Ancient Greek meaning

ἀμφιφορεύς, gen. έως, ῆος , ὁ: (φέρω, φορέω):— large jar or pitcher with two handles, of gold, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 23.92, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 24.74; of stone, **[8th c. BC]** 13.105; for wine, **[8th c. BC]** 2.290, etc.; for oil, **[6th c. AD]** Simon. 155.4: used as cinerary urn,Il.l.c. = μετρητής , **[4th c. BC]** Theopomp.Hist. 374. (The later form was ἀμφορεύς, q.v.)[^lsj]
