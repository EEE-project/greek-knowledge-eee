## Ancient Greek meaning

πατρίς, ίδος, poet. fem. of πάτριος, of one's fathers, πατρὶς αἶα, ἄρουρα, one's fatherland, country, Od. 10.236 , 20.193 ; π. γαῖα 24.322 , Hes. Sc. 1 , 12 , A. Th. 585 ; γῆ π. S. OT 641 , Ar. Th. 859 (paratrag.) ; π. πόλις the city of one's sires, Pi. O. 10(11).36 . Subst., = πάτρα 1 , Il. 5.213 , Od. 4.586 , 9.34 , Hdt. 3.140 , Th. 6.69 , etc.; κοινὴ π., i. e. the nether world, Plu. 2.113c : prov., π. γάρ ἐστι πᾶσ' ἵν' ἂν πράττῃ τις εὖ, ubi bene, ibi patria, Ar. Pl. 1151 ; simply, native town or village, UPZ 9.5 (ii B. C.), Ev.Marc. 6.1 , etc. : pl., ἐν ταῖς αὑτῶν π. D. 18.296 , cf. 305 , Pl. Plt. 308a , Hyp. Epit. 10 .[^lsj]
