## Ancient Greek meaning

 αἶψα ( ἦψα prob. in corinn. **[Boeot.]** BKT 5(2).36), Adv. quick, forthwith, on a sudden, freq. in Hom. (also αἶψα μάλα, αἶψα δ' ἔπειτα, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 4.70, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 15.193), cf. **[7th/6th c. BC]** Sapph. 1.13, **[6th c. BC]** Thgn. 663, **[6th c. BC]** Sol. 2, **[5th c. BC]** Pi. P. 4.133, **[5th c. BC]** Emp. 35.14, **[5th c. BC]** A. Supp. 481.—Poet., exc. in Mon.Ant. 18.322 (Gortyn). [^lsj]
