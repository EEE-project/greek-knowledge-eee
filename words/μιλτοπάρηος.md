## Ancient Greek meaning

μιλτο-πάρηος, ον, (παρειά) red-cheeked, epith. of ships, which had their bows painted red, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 2.637, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 9.125: Com., τρίγλη μ. Machoap. **[3rd c. AD]** Ath. 3.135b; also of a stone,Orph. L. 615; of plains, **[3rd c. AD]** Opp. C. 3.509.[^lsj]
