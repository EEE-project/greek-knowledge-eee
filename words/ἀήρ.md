## Ancient Greek meaning

Sens. 30 ; from Hdt downwds. masc. ( Il. 5.776 , 8.50 , h.Cer. 383 cannot be quoted for the masc. usage, since there πουλύς and βαθύς need not be masc.):—in Hom. and Hes. always mist, haze, not (as Aristarch. ) lower air (opp. αἰθήρ, q.v.); [ἐλάτη] μακροτάτη πεφυυῖα δι' ἠέρος αἰθέρ' ἵκανεν Il. 14.288 , cf.
