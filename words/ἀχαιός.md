## Ancient Greek meaning

Ἀχαιός, ά, όν, Achaean, Hom. , etc.: hence as Subst., Ἀχαιοί, οἱ, the Achaeans, in Hom. for the Greeks generally, Il. 2.235 , etc. Ἀχαΐα, ἡ, Achaia in Peloponnese, Th. , etc.; under the Romans, the prouince of Greece.[^lsj]
