## Ancient Greek meaning

 Ἀχαιός, ά, όν, Achaean, Hom., etc.: hence as Subst., Ἀχαιοί, οἱ, the Achaeans, in Hom. for the Greeks generally, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 2.235, etc. Ἀχαΐα, ἡ, Achaia in Peloponnese, **[5th c. BC]** Th., etc.; under the Romans, the prouince of Greece. [^lsj]
