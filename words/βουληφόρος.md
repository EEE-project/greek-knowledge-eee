## Ancient Greek meaning

βουληφόρος, ον, counselling, advising, inIl.a constant epith. of princes and leaders, **[8th c. BC]** β. ἄνδρα 2.24, al.; title of Artemis,SIG 2660.3(Milet.): also c. gen., β. Ἀχαιῶν, Τρώων, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 24.651, **[8th c. BC]** 5.180; in **[8th c. BC]** Od. 9.112οὔτ' ἀγοραὶ β. οὔτε θέμιστες, cf. **[5th c. BC]** Pi. O. 12.5: in later Prose, = βουλευτής, οἱ τοῦ μιάσματος β. **[6th c. AD]** Agath. 3.5. Adv. -ρως like a counsellor, **[4th/3rd c. BC]** Men. 123.[^lsj]
