## Ancient Greek meaning

πῖαρ, τό, only nom. and acc. (exc. dat. πίαρι Suid. ) : (πίων) :—fat, Ep. and Ion. word, βοῶν ἐκ πῖαρ ἑλέσθαι Il. 11.550 ; of men, Hp. Nat. Puer. 21 . any fatty substance, π. ἐλαίης A.R. 4.1133 ; π. μελιηδές prob. cream, AP 9.224 ( Crin. ), cf. Sol. 36.21 (πῦαρ Pap. Arist. Ath. ); thick juice from trees, Hp. Nat.Puer. 26 ; of the fig, Id. Mul. 2.205 , Ulc. 15 ; richness of soil, ἐπεὶ μάλα π. ὑπ' οὖδας Od. 9.135 , cf. h.Ap. 60 ; ἐσθλῆς ἀρούρης π. ἔγκληρον χθονός Lyc. 1060 , cf. AP 9.555 ( Crin. ). metaph., cream, choicest part of a thing, h.Ven. 30 .[^lsj]
