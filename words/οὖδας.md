## Ancient Greek meaning

οὖδας, τό, gen. οὔδεος, dat. **[8th c. BC]** οὔδει Il. 5.734, al. (more rarely **[8th c. BC]** οὔδεϊ 23.283, **[7th c. BC]** h.Merc. 284):—poet.
