## Ancient Greek meaning

κουρο-τρόφος, ον, rearing children, rare in lit. sense, γυνὴ νεοτόκος καὶ κ. Aret. CA 2.3 : usu. metaph., ἀγαθὴ κ. good nursing-mother, of Ithaca, Od. 9.27 , cf. Pi. Fr. 109 ; κ. Ἑλλάς E. Tr. 566 (lyr., s.v.l.); Ἀπόλλωνος κ., of Delos, Call. Del. 2 , 276 : freq. as epith. of goddesses, as Hecate, Hes. Th. 450 ; Ἄρτεμις D.S. 5.73 ; Λοχία Supp.Epigr. 3.400.9 (Delph., iii B. C.); of the Roman goddess Rumina, Plu. 2.278d ; esp. of Aphrodite, Hom. Epigr. 12 ; called ἡ K. alone, IG 12.840.9 , Ar. Th. 299 , Pl. Com. 174.7 , Luc. DMeretr. 5.1 ; αἱ πύλαι αἱ κατὰ Κουροτρόφον, at Delos, IG 11(2).203 A 46 (iii B. C.):—in form Κουροτρόπος, ὁ (sc. μήν), name of Acarnanian month, ib. 5(1).29.11 (Sparta).[^lsj]
