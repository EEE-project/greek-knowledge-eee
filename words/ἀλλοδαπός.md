## Ancient Greek meaning

ἀλλο-δαπός, ή, όν, (perh. ἀλλοδ-, cf. Lat. aliud, -απος = -ṇθ[υγλιδε]ος , cf. Lat. long-inquus) belonging to another people or land, foreign, **[8th c. BC]** Il. 16.550, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 17.485, **[7th/6th c. BC]** Sapph. 92, **[5th c. BC]** Pi. N. 1.22, **[5th c. BC]** A. Th. 1082, **[4th c. BC]** X. Cyr. 8.7.14, etc.;ἐν ἀλλοδαπῇ in foreign parts, Sammelb. 4284.7(iii A. D.) :— later ἀλλο-δα^πής, ές,EM 68.2, cf.Ps.-Callisth. 2.21.[^lsj]
