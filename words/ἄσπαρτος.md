## Ancient Greek meaning

ἄσπαρτος, ον, of land, unsown, untilled, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 9.123; but ἡ ἄ. the sea, **[4th c. AD]** Lib. Eth. 24.4. of plants, not sown, growing wild, **[8th c. BC]** Od. 9.109, **[3rd c. BC]** Numen.ap. **[3rd c. AD]** Ath. 9.371b.[^lsj]
