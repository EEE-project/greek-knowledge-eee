Cavafy's «Ithaka» (1911) uses the stem variant **κουβανείς** ("you carry") rather than the standard **κουβαλάς** -- "αν δεν τους κουβανείς μες στην ψυχή σου" ("if you do not carry them inside your soul"). Glossed as "carry, transport" by the official Greek Ministry of Education literature textbook's own footnote on this line, confirming the word is genuinely non-obvious even to native-speaker students, not merely an orthographic variant of κουβαλάω.[^cavafy-ithaka-kouvaneis]
