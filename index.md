- [grammar/](grammar/index.md) — Grammatical Rules, one per rule -- scoped to one period, or a change between periods
- [culture/](culture/index.md) — Cultural Context entries, one per person/theme/work
- [texts/](texts/index.md) — Literary Text (the original) and Literary Translation entries, per work
- [references/](references/index.md) — how this KB uses each of its sources
