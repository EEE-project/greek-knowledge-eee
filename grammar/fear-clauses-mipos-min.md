## φοβάμαι takes three different clause types for three different senses

**φοβάμαι** "to fear, be afraid" (and near-synonyms **ανησυχώ** "to worry," **έχω αγωνία** "to be anxious") governs three distinct constructions, each with a different meaning. With **ότι/πως** + indicative, it states a near-certainty about a bad outcome: **Φοβάμαι ότι θα χάσουμε το τρένο.** "I'm afraid we're going to miss the train" (I'm nearly sure of it).[^ellinika-b-ch12] With **μήπως** or **μη(ν)** + a bare future or aorist stem, it expresses worried uncertainty about a possible bad outcome: **Φοβάμαι μήπως χάσουμε το τρένο.** "I'm worried we might miss the train" (not sure, but anxious).[^ellinika-b-ch12] With plain **να**, it expresses fear of the *subject's own* action: **Φοβάμαι να ανεβώ στη μηχανή του.** "I'm afraid to get on his motorbike" (I myself am reluctant).[^ellinika-b-ch12]

**μη(ν)/μήπως** clauses of apprehension follow the same pattern after other verbs of worry too, not just φοβάμαι: **Ανησυχώ μήπως δεν έρθει.** "I'm worried he might not come."[^ellinika-b-ch12]
