## General location: only σε, refined by adverbs when it needs to be specific

Modern Greek expresses "where something is" in general with a single preposition, **σε** ("at/in/on"), taking the accusative and usually contracting with the following article: **στο, στην, στον, στους**. Where English or Russian distinguishes "on," "in," and "at" by preposition choice, Greek leaves that job to context, or to an adverb of place layered on top when the sentence would otherwise be ambiguous.[^livexp-grammar]

- **Είναι στο τραπέζι μου.** -- ambiguous on its own: "on my table" or "in my table (drawer)."
- **Είναι μέσα στο τραπέζι μου.** -- "(specifically) inside my table."
- **Είναι πάνω στο τραπέζι μου.** -- "(specifically) on top of my table."

Common adverbs of place used this way include **δίπλα (σε)** "next to," **κοντά (σε)** "near," **μακριά από** "far from," **απέναντι από** "opposite," **μπροστά από / πίσω από** "in front of / behind," **μέσα σε / έξω από** "in / out of," **πάνω από / κάτω από** "above / under," and **ανάμεσα σε ... και ...** "between ... and ...."[^livexp-grammar]

- **Η ταβέρνα είναι δίπλα στον φούρνο.** -- "The tavern is next to the bakery."
- **Το κινητό είναι μέσα στην τσάντα.** -- "The phone is inside the bag."

Before a noun, an adverb of place takes a following preposition (usually **σε** or **από**) governing that noun; with no noun after it the adverb stands alone: **Υπάρχει κανένας σταθμός του μετρό εδώ κοντά;** "Is there a metro station near here?"[^livexp-grammar][^ellinika-b-ch3] The textbook lists the place adverbs **κοντά, μακριά, μέσα, έξω, δεξιά, αριστερά, απέναντι, δίπλα, ανάμεσα** and practises several of them with **από** (**απέναντι από**, **πίσω από**, **μπροστά από**, **έξω από**).[^ellinika-a]

All of the above describes static location -- where something *is*. A destination is usually given with **σε** as well: **Πάω στον Πειραιά.** "I'm going to Piraeus."[^ellinika-a] The preposition **προς** "toward, in the direction of" names a direction of movement rather than a place reached: **Οδηγώ προς την Πάφο.** "I'm driving toward Paphos."[^livexp-grammar]
