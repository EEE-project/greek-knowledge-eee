## Obligation's own time shifts on πρέπει, not on the να-clause

When **πρέπει** "must, should" governs a [να-clause](subjunctive-triggers-and-ambiguity.md), shifting the obligation itself into the past or future changes only **πρέπει**, not the dependent subjunctive verb: **Έπρεπε να φύγω.** "I had to leave" (past), **Πρέπει να φύγω.** "I must leave" (now), **Θα πρέπει να φύγω.** "I'll have to leave" (later) -- **να φύγω** stays identical across all three.[^ellinika-b-ch11]

This matches how **να**-clauses behave generally: the subjunctive verb form is a non-past form with no tense contrast of its own, only an aspect contrast (see [subjunctive-simple-vs-continuous](subjunctive-simple-vs-continuous.md)), and the **να**-clause took over many functions of Ancient Greek's lost infinitive (see [infinitive-loss](infinitive-loss.md)) -- so the time reference comes from the governing verb.[^wikipedia-mg-grammar]
