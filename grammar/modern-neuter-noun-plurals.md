## Neuter plurals: the article and the ending both move to -α

Modern Greek neuter nouns take the plural article **τα** in both nominative and accusative, and the noun's own ending shifts too, with stress staying on the same syllable as the singular. Three endings cover most common vocabulary: **-ο → -α** (**το βιβλίο → τα βιβλία**, **το τηλέφωνο → τα τηλέφωνα**), **-ι → -ια** (**το παιδί → τα παιδιά**, **το σπίτι → τα σπίτια**), and **-μα → -ματα** (**το γράμμα → τα γράμματα**, **το πράγμα → τα πράγματα**).[^livexp-grammar]

Loanwords borrowed into the neuter gender are often indeclinable and keep the same form in the plural: **το μπαρ → τα μπαρ**.[^livexp-grammar] Compound nouns generally form their plural by the rule for the compound's own final element, not the whole word's surface shape.[^livexp-grammar]
