## Reporting a question: keep the question word, or add αν

A direct question introduced by a question word (**τι, πώς, πότε, γιατί, πού**...) is reported indirectly by keeping that same word and dropping the question mark, embedding it as a clause: **«Πότε φεύγεις;»** → **Ο Φοίβος ρώτησε τη θεία του πότε φεύγει.** "Phoivos asked his aunt when she was leaving."[^ellinika-b-ch9] A direct yes/no question, which has no question word to keep, is instead introduced by **αν** "whether": **«Υπάρχει κανένας κάδος ανακύκλωσης;»** → **Ο Πάμπλο ρώτησε αν υπάρχει κανένας κάδος ανακύκλωσης.** "Pamplo asked whether there was a recycling bin."[^ellinika-b-ch9]

Reporting a question isn't just a punctuation change: person and possessive reference shift the same way they would in reported statements generally, from the original speaker's perspective to the reporting speaker's -- a direct **«...θα πηγαίνεις...»** (2nd person, addressed to "you") becomes indirect **...θα πηγαίνει...** (3rd person, about "her") once it's embedded in someone else's report.[^ellinika-b-ch9]
