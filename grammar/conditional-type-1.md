## Real conditionals: αν takes the bare future stem, no θα

A Type 1 ("real"/likely) conditional pairs **αν** ("if") plus the simple-future stem *without* **θα** in the if-clause, with an ordinary **θα**-marked simple future in the result clause -- the same stem covered in [future-tha-aspect](future-tha-aspect.md), just missing its usual particle: **Αν βρέξει αύριο, δε θα πάμε για περπάτημα.** "If it rains tomorrow, we won't go for a walk."[^ellinika-b-ch8]

- **Αν αρχίσεις γυμναστική, θα νιώσεις πολύ καλύτερα.** "If you start exercising, you'll feel much better."
- **Αν διαβάσεις περισσότερο, θα περάσεις στις εξετάσεις.** "If you study more, you'll pass the exams."

The if-clause's verb form is easy to mistake for a subjunctive **να**-clause form, since both use the identical bare future/dependent stem -- the difference is purely which particle introduces it, **αν** here versus **να** in a dependent clause (see [na-dependent-verb-forms](na-dependent-verb-forms.md)).
