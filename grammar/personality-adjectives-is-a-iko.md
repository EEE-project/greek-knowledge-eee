## A fourth adjective pattern, mostly for character traits: -ης / -α / -ικο

Beyond the patterns already covered in [adjectives-ending-ys](adjectives-ending-ys.md) and [color-adjectives-ending-is](color-adjectives-ending-is.md), a fourth declension shape -- masculine **-ης**, feminine **-α**, neuter **-ικο** -- covers a large, productive family of informal adjectives describing personality traits and physical characteristics: **γκρινιάρης, γκρινιάρα, γκρινιάρικο** "grumpy," **τεμπέλης, τεμπέλα, τεμπέλικο** "lazy," **ζηλιάρης, ζηλιάρα, ζηλιάρικο** "jealous."[^ellinika-b-ch8] The masculine plural takes the same **-ηδες** ending already covered in [nouns-plural-in-des](nouns-plural-in-des.md) (**οι γκρινιάρηδες**), confirming that pattern isn't limited to nouns.[^ellinika-b-ch8]

Many words in this family are built with a recognizable descriptive suffix on top of an ordinary noun or verb stem, and the pattern stays productive for compound physical-description words too: **κοκκινομάλλης** "red-haired" (κόκκινος + μαλλιά), **γαλανομάτης** "blue-eyed" (γαλανός + μάτια).[^ellinika-b-ch8]

- **Ο Γιάννης πάλι δεν πλήρωσε τον λογαριασμό. Είναι πολύ ξεχασιάρης.** "Yannis didn't pay the bill again. He's really forgetful."
