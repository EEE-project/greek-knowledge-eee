## Prefixes shift a verb's meaning in regular, learnable ways

Modern Greek attaches a fairly small set of prefixes productively to verbs (and related nouns/adjectives) to shift their meaning, mostly continuing prefixes already active in Ancient Greek. Negating/privative prefixes: **α-/αν-** "without, un-" (**γνωστός** known → **άγνωστος** unknown; **ανεξάρτητος** independent); **δυσ-** "bad, hard to" (**τύχη** luck → **δυστυχία** misfortune).[^livexp-grammar]

Reversal/undoing: **ξε-** "un-, dis-" (**ντύνω** dress → **ξεντύνω** undress; **κλειδώνω** lock → **ξεκλειδώνω** unlock).[^livexp-grammar] Intensive/excessive: **κατα-** "completely, down" (**στρέφω** turn → **καταστρέφω** destroy utterly; **γράφω** write → **καταγράφω** record in full); **υπερ-** "over-, super-" (**θερμαίνω** heat → **υπερθερμαίνω** overheat); **παρα-** "over-, to excess" (**κοιμάμαι** sleep → **παρακοιμάμαι** oversleep; **τρώω** eat → **παρατρώω** overeat).[^livexp-grammar]

Spatial/directional: **ανα-** "up, again" (**βαίνω** go → **αναβαίνω** go up); **δια-** "through, across" (**περνώ** pass → **διαπερνώ** pass through); **υπο-** "under" (**γράφω** write → **υπογράφω** sign); **προ-** "before" (**βλέπω** see → **προβλέπω** foresee); **μετα-** "after, change" (**φράζω** express → **μεταφράζω** translate); **απο-** "away from" (**κόπτω** cut → **αποκόπτω** cut off); **επι-** "on, over" (**γράφω** write → **επιγράφω** inscribe).[^livexp-grammar]

**ξανά-** is different from the rest: it comes from the adverb **ξανά** "again" and attaches directly to the verb (like English *re-*): **γράφω** write → **ξαναγράφω** write again.[^livexp-grammar]
