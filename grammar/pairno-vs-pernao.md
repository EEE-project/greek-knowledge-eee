## παίρνω vs. περνάω: taking transport, and passing by

**παίρνω** and **περνάω/περνώ** are easy to confuse in transit contexts, but mean different things. **παίρνω** ("to take") covers taking a means of transport or picking someone up: **παίρνω το λεωφορείο** "I take the bus," **παίρνω τα παιδιά από το σχολείο** "I pick the kids up from school."[^ellinika-b-ch3] **περνάω/περνώ** ("to pass, go by") describes something moving past a point, not being taken: **Πόσο συχνά περνάει το λεωφορείο;** "How often does the bus go by/come?"[^ellinika-b-ch3]
