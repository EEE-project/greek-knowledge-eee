## θα splits from its verb to let the weak pronoun through

A weak object clitic (see [weak-pronouns-position](weak-pronouns-position.md)) governed by a **θα**-future verb slots in between **θα** and the verb itself, not before **θα** and not after the verb: **Θα διαβάσουν την εφημερίδα.** → **Θα τη διαβάσουν.** "They'll read it."[^ellinika-b-ch8]

The same placement holds for an indirect-object clitic, and for negation, which itself precedes **θα**: **Θα σας πω κάτι.** "I'll tell you something"; **Δε θα το ανοίξουν.** "They won't open it" -- **δε(ν)** before **θα**, the clitic still directly before the verb.[^ellinika-b-ch8]
