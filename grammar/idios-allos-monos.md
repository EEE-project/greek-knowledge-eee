## ο ίδιος, άλλος, μόνος: three adjective-like pronouns of identity

**ο ίδιος / η ίδια / το ίδιο** marks identity -- "the same," or, referring back to the subject, an emphatic "myself/yourself/himself...": asked for by name, a man confirms **Ο ίδιος.** "Speaking" (literally "the same one"); **Έχουμε τους ίδιους καθηγητές.** "We have the same teachers."[^ellinika-b-ch3]

**άλλος / άλλη / άλλο** "other, another" changes meaning with word order around **ένας**: **ένα άλλο ποτήρι** is a *different* glass (not this one), while **άλλο ένα ποτήρι** is one *more* glass -- the same two words, reversed, giving genuinely different meanings.[^ellinika-b-ch3]

**μόνος, μόνη, μόνο** "alone" takes an enclitic possessive to specify whose solitude is meant, agreeing in gender/number with the person alone: **Η Μαρίνα μένει μόνη της.** "Marina lives alone (by herself)," **Θα πάμε μόνες μας.** "We'll go by ourselves (fem.)."[^ellinika-b-ch3]
