## τέτοιος "like this/that kind" and τόσος "this much/many"

**τέτοιος / τέτοια / τέτοιο** means "such (a), like this/that one" -- literally "like this/that" (**= όπως αυτός/αυτή/αυτό**), inflecting as an ordinary adjective: **Πρώτη φορά γνωρίζω τέτοιον άνθρωπο.** "I've never met a person like this before," **Τέτοιοι οδηγοί είναι επικίνδυνοι.** "Drivers like that are dangerous."[^ellinika-b-ch3]

**τόσος / τόση / τόσο** means "this/that much, so many," expressing degree or quantity: **Είχε τόσο κόσμο, που φύγαμε.** "There were so many people that we left," and standing alone as an answer to a quantity question: **– Πόση ζάχαρη θέλεις; – Τόση.** "– How much sugar? – This much."[^ellinika-b-ch3]

Both inflect as ordinary 1st/2nd-declension adjectives across gender, number, and case, agreeing with whatever noun they modify.[^ellinika-b-ch3]
