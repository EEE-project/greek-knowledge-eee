## Adverbs that call for the continuous, not the simple, future

Certain adverbial time expressions signal that a future statement is about an ongoing habit or duration rather than a single event, and so normally take the continuous future -- **θα** + unchanged present stem, as already covered in [future-tha-aspect](future-tha-aspect.md) -- rather than the simple future. Expressions marking an open-ended new habit starting from some point: **από αύριο** "from tomorrow (on)," **από 'δω και πέρα** "from now on." Expressions of ongoing frequency: **πάντα, συχνά, τακτικά** "always, often, regularly," **σπάνια** "rarely," **κάπου κάπου** "every now and then."[^ellinika-b-ch9]

- **Από αύριο θα ανακυκλώνω.** "From tomorrow on, I'll be recycling."
- **Θα παίρνω το τραμ.** "I'll be taking the tram (as a new regular habit)."
