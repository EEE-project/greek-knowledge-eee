## κάποιος/κάτι: an unspecified "someone/something," no negative reading

Unlike [kanenas-tipota](kanenas-tipota.md), **κάποιος/κάποια/κάποιο** ("someone," literally "= ένας/μια/ένα") and the indeclinable **κάτι** ("something") only ever mean a genuine, positive "some-," never flipping to a negative sense: **Κάποιος σε ζητάει.** "Someone's asking for you," **Ήθελα να σου πω κάτι.** "I wanted to tell you something."[^ellinika-b-ch3] **κάποιος** inflects fully for gender, number, and case like an ordinary pronoun/adjective (**κάποιου, κάποιον, κάποιων**...), while **κάτι** never inflects at all.[^ellinika-b-ch3]

**κάτι** also has a colloquial "and a bit more" use after a round number: **– Τι ώρα είναι; – Πέντε και κάτι.** "– What time is it? – Just after five."[^ellinika-b-ch3]
