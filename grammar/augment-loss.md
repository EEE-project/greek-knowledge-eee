## Unstressed augment loss in Modern Greek

κοινή's past tenses (imperfect, aorist, pluperfect) mark past time with a prefixed **augment**, normally **ἐ-**, added whenever the stem's own first vowel wouldn't otherwise be accented: κοινή imperfect **ἔλυον**, aorist **ἔλυσα**.[^atticist-koine-tenses] Modern Greek keeps this augment only when it would carry the word's stress; an unstressed augment is simply dropped. So κοινή's **ἔλυον** ("I was loosing") survives into Modern Greek as **έλυνα**, its augment retained because the stress still falls there, while a form whose stress moves elsewhere in the paradigm drops the augment entirely.[^atticist-koine-tenses]
