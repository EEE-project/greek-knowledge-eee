## Unstressed augment loss in Modern Greek

κοινή's past tenses (imperfect, aorist, pluperfect) mark past time with a prefixed **augment**, normally **ἐ-**, on every indicative form of the paradigm, whether or not it carries the stress: imperfect **ἔλυον** and **ἐλύομεν**, aorist **ἔλυσα** and **ἐλύσαμεν**.[^atticist-koine-tenses] Modern Greek usually drops an unstressed augment: **έλυνα** keeps its stressed **έ-**, while the same paradigm has **λύναμε**, and the aorist has **έλυσα** beside **λύσαμε**.[^atticist-koine-tenses] In regular verbs the past-tense stress falls on the third syllable from the end, and the prefix is inserted only when the form without it would have one or two syllables (*λύσα* → **έλυσα**, but **λύσαμε** already has three).[^wikipedia-mg-grammar]
