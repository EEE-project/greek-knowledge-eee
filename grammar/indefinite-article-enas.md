## The indefinite article: forms of ένας

Modern Greek's indefinite article is an inflected form of the numeral **ένας** "one" -- introducing one non-specific countable noun, agreeing with it in gender and case: **ένας φίλος**, **μία γυναίκα**, **ένα βιβλίο**.[^livexp-grammar]

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nominative | ένας | μία | ένα |
| Accusative | έναν / ένα | μία | ένα |
| Genitive | ενός | μίας | ενός |

The masculine accusative has two forms, **έναν** and **ένα**: the appended **-ν** is the same movable-ν principle this corpus already documents for Ancient Greek's [ἐστίν and the 3rd-plural **-ουσι(ν)**](movable-nu-and-enclitics.md) -- word-final ν surfacing before a vowel or a pause, dropped before a consonant -- carried on into Modern Greek and extended to new words it never applied to classically, ένας among them.[^livexp-grammar]
