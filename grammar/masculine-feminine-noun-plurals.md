## Masculine and feminine plurals: one true exception, -ος

Masculine nouns in **-ας** or **-ης** and all feminine nouns share one plural pattern: nominative and accusative plural are identical in the noun itself (**-ες**), and only the article distinguishes case (**οι** vs. **τους/τις**) -- **ο εργάτης → οι εργάτες / τους εργάτες**, **η γυναίκα → οι γυναίκες / τις γυναίκες**.[^livexp-grammar]

Masculine nouns in **-ος** are the real exception: their plural ending itself differs by case, matching the article's own vowel -- nominative **οι φίλοι**, accusative **τους φίλους**.[^livexp-grammar]

A smaller class of feminine nouns, historically continuing the Ancient Greek 3rd declension **-ις**, forms its plural in **-εις** rather than **-ες**: **η πόλη → οι πόλεις / τις πόλεις**.[^livexp-grammar]
