# Homer/Odyssey scholarship

Personal-library reference works on Homer and the Odyssey/Iliad,
candidate citation sources for this KB's Odyssey course content
(`words/*.md` files derived from the Odyssey vocabulary) and any future
Iliad coverage.

- Голинкевич Н.Т. (1996), *Читаем Гомера: Одиссея, Песнь 1-я.
  Комментарий* — line-by-line philological commentary on Odyssey Book 1.
- Гордезиани Р.В., *Проблемы гомеровского эпоса* (Problems of Homeric
  Epic).
- Сахарный Н.Л., *Илиада: Разыскания в области смысла и стиля
  гомеровской поэмы* (Iliad: studies in the meaning and style of
  Homeric poetry).
- Lord, Albert B., *The Singer of Tales* (Russian: *Сказитель*) — the
  foundational study of oral-formulaic composition, applied to Homer.
- Тахо-Годи А.А. (ed.), *Античные гимны* (Ancient Hymns) — edited
  collection of ancient Greek hymnic poetry, including the Homeric
  Hymns.

**Status: not integrated.** Print/scanned works, not structured data —
documented here as citation sources for Cultural Context and
commentary-level content, not pipeline inputs.
