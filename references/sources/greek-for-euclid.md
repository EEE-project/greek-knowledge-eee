# Greek For Euclid (Calvert)

`https://mysite.du.edu/~etuttle/classics/nugreek/contents.htm` — a
26-lesson course by J. B. Calvert (a University of Denver personal page;
composed 2000, revised 2002) teaching exactly the Ancient Greek needed to
read Euclid's *Elements* in the original: lessons 1–8 cover grammar
foundations (alphabet, cases, declensions, verbs, particles), 9–18 apply
that grammar to Euclidean conventions (propositions, definitions,
postulates, axioms), and 19–26 work through specific propositions from
the *Elements* and other Euclidean texts. Unlike every other source in
this list, it targets mathematical/technical Greek vocabulary
specifically rather than general, epic, or Koine usage.

**Status: not integrated.** A set of static HTML pages with no bulk
download or API. Greek text is rendered in the legacy pre-Unicode
**SPIonic** font encoding (a separate font download from Scholars Press
is required just to display it correctly) rather than plain Unicode
Greek — programmatic extraction would need a SPIonic→Unicode glyph
mapping first, a conversion step none of this KB's other sources
require. No license is stated. Candidate as a curated citation source
for a future mathematical-Greek or Euclid-focused Grammatical Rule /
Cultural Context entry, not a bulk-ingestible dataset.
