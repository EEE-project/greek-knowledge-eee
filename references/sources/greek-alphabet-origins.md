# Greek alphabet / writing-system origins

A collection of academic papers on the origins and formation of the
Greek alphabet and its pre-alphabetic antecedents (Linear A/B) —
candidate citation sources for Cultural Context / historical-linguistics
content on the alphabet's own history, a topic not covered by any other
source in this list.

- "The formation of the Greek alphabet" (2022) — traces the family tree
  of early Greek alphabets; primary source Jeffery, *The Local Scripts
  of Archaic Greece* (1961/1990).
- Kenanidis, I.K., "A Comparative Linguistic Study about the [Greek
  alphabet]" — *Scholars Journal of Arts, Humanities and Social
  Sciences* 3(1E), 2015.
- Ragousi, Eirene N., "Hellenic Alphabet: Its Origins, Use, and Early
  Functions."
- Chotalishvili, Lela (Tbilisi), "Formation of the Greek Writing Systems
  in Ancient Greece in the Context [of...]" — *Phasis* 15–16, 2012–2013.
- "Greek Writing Systems" — chapter in *Encyclopedia of Ancient Greek
  Language and Linguistics*, Vol. 2 (G–O), ed. G. Giannakis.
- Davis, Brent (University of Melbourne), "Introduction to the Aegean
  Pre-Alphabetic Scripts."
- "Mother or Sister? Rethinking the origins [of the Greek alphabet]" —
  in *Understanding Relations Between Scripts II*, Oxbow Books, open
  access.
- Carrasquer Vidal, Miguel, "The Greek alphabet" (October 2004).
- Mosenkis, Iurii, "The Greek Written Language 2000–1500 BCE: Linear A."

**Status: not integrated.** Academic PDFs, not structured data —
documented here as citation sources for alphabet/script-history content.
