# Digital Encyclopedia of Atticism (DEA)

A research database (`https://atticism.eu/`) documenting ancient Greek
linguistic purism through Atticist lexica — specialized ancient
"dictionaries" prescribing which Greek words/forms to use or avoid
relative to the Classical Attic standard. Three sections:

- Lexicographic entries from Atticist lexica, with search and reference
  editions.
- Manuscripts & Editions — medieval and early-modern manuscript records
  and printed editions.
- Scholars & Works — ancient and Byzantine contributors to the Atticist
  tradition.

Maintained by the PURA project (Ca' Foscari University of Venice,
2021–2026, led by Prof. Olga Tribulato), funded by the European Research
Council under Horizon 2020. Open access, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

**Status: not integrated.** Includes structured search across entries/
manuscripts/scholars, but no bulk export or API was checked — documented
here as a candidate source for register/usage-correctness commentary
(which forms were considered "proper Attic" vs. avoided), a dimension
none of this KB's other sources cover directly.
