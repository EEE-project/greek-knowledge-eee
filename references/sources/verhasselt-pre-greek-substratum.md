# Verhasselt, "The Pre-Greek Linguistic Substratum: An Overview of Current Research" (2009)

Gertjan Verhasselt, published in *Les Études Classiques* 77(3–4), 2009
(`https://www.lesetudesclassiques.be/index.php/lec/article/view/62`). A
peer-reviewed literature review of scholarship on the Pre-Greek substrate
question — the competing accounts of what non-Indo-European language(s)
Ancient Greek borrowed roughly 1,000 words from before the arrival of
Greek speakers, including the pre-IE "Aegean"/"Pelasgian" substrate
hypothesis and its rivals.

journal page); no explicit Creative Commons or other reuse license is
