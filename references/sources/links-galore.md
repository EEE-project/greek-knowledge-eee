# Links Galore (free scans of classical text series)

A published Google sheet that lists links to free digital copies of the
volumes of classical text series, mostly on the Internet Archive, some on
HathiTrust and Google Books. It has one tab per series. Among them are the
Loeb Classical Library (one row per Loeb number, with later printings in
the rows below it), the Bibliotheca Teubneriana (by author), the Oxford
Classical Texts, the Collection Budé (série grecque), Stephanus'
*Thesaurus Graecae Linguae*, Migne's *Patrologia Graeca* and the *Corpus
Scriptorum Historiae Byzantinae*.

- Sheet: <https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vRkUFBfVVqv5Tr2aZS4apFNpTJ-ys6VqeQxgsAI1v7cH5putIgchYWJAVGHuu0lWGmdD2DU7Vb1o7XH/pubhtml>
- Download: the same address with `pub?output=xlsx` in place of `pubhtml`
  gives the whole workbook (33 tabs on 2026-11-18). Some tab names end
  with a space.
- The sheet is linked from the Loeb row of T. Kenny's table of bilingual
  classical series:
  <https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1OGFA-vSmu7yTZutb9f7uVZGT2mWkKYP1sWO2-I3huQQ>.
- A link marked `Xº` is an Internet Archive lending copy (borrow only):
  the two checked on 2026-11-18 are access-restricted. The links marked
  `X` that were checked (Murray's *Odyssey* vol. I, Allen's Oxford text
  vol. III) are free to read and to download.
