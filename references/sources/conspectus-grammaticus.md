# CONSPECTVS GRAMMATICVS (Palaestra reference grammar)

Palaestra's (Sodalitas Litterarum) own trilingual (Latin/Greek/Russian)
systematic reference-grammar handout, issued in five short installments:
Volumes I-IV plus a separate "aoristus II" supplement. This is the actual
"Grammatica di consultazione" Athenaze's own chapter text repeatedly
points students to by section number for fuller detail — confirmed by the
Palaestra/Sodalitas Litterarum logo on every page. No individual named
author.

**Status: integrated (Volumes I-III only).** Local files, not fetched
live:
- `lectures/ancient_greek/CONSPECTVS GRAMMATICVS I_graecus.pdf` — the
  article, full 2nd-declension paradigm (masc./fem./neut., 4 accent
  classes), gender-assignment rules, vocative irregularities, present
  indicative active (pure/contract-έω/εἰμί, with reconstructed
  proto-forms).
- `lectures/Palaestra/ancient_greek.2026.summer/CONSPECTVS GRAMMATICVS
  II_graecus.pdf` — present imperative active, same three verb classes,
  plus six compound-εἰμί verbs.
- `lectures/ancient_greek/CONSPECTVS GRAMMATICVS III_graecus.pdf` —
  1st/2nd-declension adjectives (3-termination, 2-termination) and the
  μέγας/πολύς suppletive paradigm.

Cited (dual-sourced alongside Athenaze) in
`grammar/second-declension-masc-neut.md`,
`grammar/third-singular-present-indicative.md`,
`grammar/movable-nu-and-enclitics.md`,
`grammar/present-imperative-active.md`, and
`grammar/adjective-declension-and-suppletion.md`.

**Confirmed out of scope:**
`lectures/ancient_greek/CONSPECTVS GRAMMATICVS IV_graecus.pdf` (1st-
declension feminine nouns, -άω contract verbs) and
`lectures/ancient_greek/CONSPECTVS GRAMMATICVS_aoristus II.pdf` (2nd-aorist
paradigm) — both genuinely later-chapter material (Athenaze's own Ch.1
vocabulary is masculine-only by its own text; no aorist appears until
well past Ch.2). Will become relevant to whatever future pass covers
Athenaze Ch.3+, on the same schema.

**Authority:** `linguistic: 4` — mainstream, uncontroversial classical
Greek grammar, systematically presented, checkable against any standard
reference grammar; no factual errors found in the three volumes actually
reviewed. Not a 5: unpublished, no individually named/credentialed
author, so there's no independent scholarly reputation to point to —
only Palaestra's own institutional continuity as a real, identifiable
teaching organization.
