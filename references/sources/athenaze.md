# Athenaze: Introduzione al greco antico

M. Balme, G. Lawall, L. Miraglia, T. F. Bórri (Edizioni Accademia
Vivarium Novum) — the Italian-language edition of Balme & Lawall's
widely-used introductory Ancient Greek reading course, adapted and
expanded by Miraglia and Bórri for Accademia Vivarium Novum, a
Latin/Greek immersion institution. Two volumes, narrative-based (the
fictional Athenian farmer Dikaiopolis and his family), building from the
alphabet through to real excerpts of Homer, Herodotus, Thucydides, and
Aristophanes by the end of the course.

**Status: integrated.** Vol. I, Ch. 1-2 sourced (two independent
transcriptions merged — see the `athenaze-ch1`/`athenaze-ch1-ext`/
`athenaze-ch2`/`athenaze-ch2-ext` source IDs in `okfbuild/pilot_content.py`)
for grammar-rule pedagogical framing/examples throughout this pass's
Grammatical Rule content, and for its own cultural essays (Peloponnesian
War setting, Athenian farmer class system, Dikaiopolis/Aristophanes'
*Acharnians*, slavery in Athens) — see `culture/` for the individual
entries.

**Authority:** `linguistic: 4` — institutionally published by a serious
Classics-education press, written by established pedagogues; simplifies
for teaching but does not misstate grammar. Not a 5: explicitly a
graded-reader textbook, not a scholarly reference grammar in its own
right (that role belongs to a source like CONSPECTVS GRAMMATICVS or a
full academic reference grammar). `historical: 3` — its cultural essays
are competent secondary synthesis aimed at students, not original
historical scholarship; where the text directly quotes a primary source
(Thucydides, Aristotle) within its own prose, that quoted content's
authority is the primary source's own, not Athenaze's.
