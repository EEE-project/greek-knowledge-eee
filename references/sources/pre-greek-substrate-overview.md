# Pre-Greek substrate — overview (Hieber)

A long-form post on *Linguistic Discovery*, a weekly linguistics
newsletter/blog run by Daniel W. Hieber, Ph.D. (`linguisticdiscovery.com`).
Surveys the ~1,000 Ancient Greek words judged to lack Indo-European
etymologies, the methods used to identify them as borrowed (phonological
shapes impossible for inherited PIE vocabulary, semantic domains typical
of substrate borrowing, variant pronunciations of the same word), the
recurring Pre-Greek suffix patterns (`-nth-`, `-ss-`/`-tt-`, `-mn-`,
`-n-`), and Minoan cultural influence on Greek vocabulary. Links out to
this same author's toponyms dataset (see
[`references/sources/pre-greek-toponyms.md`](pre-greek-toponyms.md)) and a
bibliography of scholarly sources.
