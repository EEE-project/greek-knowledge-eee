## Dikaiopolis's name, and Aristophanes's Acharnians

The protagonist's name, **Δικαιόπολις**, is built from **δίκαιος** "just" and **πόλις** "city, state" — roughly "just citizen" or "one who lives in a just city."[^athenaze-ch1]

Dikaiopolis is also the protagonist of Aristophanes's comedy *Acharnians*, first staged in 426 BC — the play Athenaze's own course draws its final readings from, where Dikaiopolis appears as a peacemaker.[^athenaze-ch1]
